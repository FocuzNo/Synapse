CloudDesk Knowledge Base

Getting started

CloudDesk is a fictional SaaS workspace for handling internal support requests, shared inbox triage, and lightweight customer operations. New customers begin by creating a workspace, inviting teammates, and connecting their primary support inbox. During the first login, workspace owners should review company details in Settings, create a chatbot, and upload one or more plain text knowledge files before activating the assistant. The chatbot can answer only from uploaded knowledge, so empty workspaces should expect fallback answers until at least one document reaches the ready state.

Inviting team members

Workspace owners can invite teammates from the Team section in CloudDesk settings. To invite a teammate, open Settings, choose Team, click Invite member, and enter the teammate's work email address. Invited users receive an email with a secure invitation link and must accept it before they appear as active members. Owners can assign the Admin or Member role during the invitation flow. Admins can update workspace settings and manage chatbot content, while Members can view shared data but cannot change billing or workspace ownership.

Subscription cancellation

CloudDesk subscriptions can be cancelled from the Billing page by the workspace owner. When a subscription is cancelled, the plan remains active until the end of the current billing period and then downgrades to Free automatically. Cancellation does not delete chatbots or knowledge files immediately, but premium features such as branding removal and analytics access become unavailable after the downgrade takes effect. If a customer needs help before cancelling, support recommends exporting key data and reviewing plan limits to avoid unexpected restrictions after downgrade.

Slack integration

CloudDesk supports a Slack integration for workspace notifications. Owners or Admins can open Integrations, choose Slack, and authorize the CloudDesk app for a selected workspace. After setup, CloudDesk can post alerts for new priority tickets, teammate mentions, and automation failures to the chosen Slack channel. If Slack is disconnected, CloudDesk stops sending notifications until the integration is reconnected. CloudDesk does not use Slack as a login provider in this setup, and chatbot knowledge is not read from Slack messages.

Two-factor authentication

CloudDesk supports two-factor authentication for all email and password accounts. Users can enable two-factor authentication from their personal Security settings by scanning a TOTP QR code with an authenticator app and confirming a one-time code. After activation, CloudDesk requires the time-based code during future sign-ins on new sessions. Recovery codes should be stored in a secure password manager. Workspace owners can require two-factor authentication for every teammate on paid plans, but the Free plan leaves it optional per user.

Support contact

CloudDesk support is available Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 UTC. Customers can contact support by emailing support@clouddesk.example or by opening the in-app Help panel and choosing Contact support. Urgent billing issues are prioritized, while feature requests are reviewed during weekly product triage. If a chatbot answer is incomplete, support recommends updating the knowledge files with the missing policy details and trying the question again after the document finishes processing.
